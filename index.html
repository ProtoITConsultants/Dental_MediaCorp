Is DMC right for you?

Would we make a good fit?

Truth is, we’re not the right partner for every clinic. And that’s intentional. We work best with practices that value quality, alignment and long-term growth. Here’s how to know if DMC is the right fit for you.

We will help

We’re the right partner for clinics who…

  • Value an all-in-one marketing solutionEliminating the need to juggle multiple vendors for website, social, photography, SEO, paid ads and appointment setting.
  • Want more patient flow, especially high-value casesIdeal for clinics looking to attract implants, full-arch, Invisalign, sedation and comprehensive dentistry.
  • Are committed to consistent, qualified patient systemsTeams who understand marketing works best when paired with proper follow-up, communication and patient experience.
  • Are open to cohesive, innovative marketing effortsClinics willing to collaborate, implement and engage with a streamlined, under-one-roof provider.
  • Understand comprehensive treatment & lifetime valueFocused on retention, book-back rates, annual value and long-term patient relationships, not just one-off visits.
We won’t help

We’re not the right fit for practices who…

  • Want “quick fix” or piecemeal marketingIf you’re looking for a logo here, a random ad there or patchwork services, we’re not the right partner.
  • Prefer managing multiple vendors independentlyIf you want to micromanage separate teams for SEO, ads, site, content and social, our integrated model isn’t for you.
  • Aren’t ready to invest in patient-centered growthIf a clinic isn’t prepared to improve systems, communication or follow-up, marketing performance will always plateau.
  • Expect results without internal alignmentIf the team doesn’t respond to leads or adopt simple workflow adjustments, no marketing partner can succeed.
  • Are seeking untested marketing supportClinics wanting to work with new brands or products that have yet to be tested in the marketplace.
